Transmission Control Protocol / Internet Protocol

TCP/IP is the foundational "language" of the internet, acting as the universal rulebook for digital communication. TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) is responsible for breaking data into small packets and ensuring they arrive safely and in the correct order. Meanwhile, IP (Internet Protocol) acts like a digital GPS, addressing each packet so it knows exactly which computer in the world it needs to reach. Together, they form the backbone of every email, video stream, and web page view.
