Web 3.0: The Semantic and Decentralized Web

Web 3.0 aims to solve the issues of data privacy and central control found in Web 2.0. By utilizing blockchain technology, Web 3.0 envisions a "decentralized" internet where users own their data and digital assets. Furthermore, the "Semantic Web" aspect uses artificial intelligence to help machines understand content just as humans do, resulting in a more intuitive, personalized, and secure browsing experience.
