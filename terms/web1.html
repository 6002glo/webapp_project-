Web 1.0: The Static Web

Web 1.0 represents the first generation of the World Wide Web, often described as the "read-only" era. During this time, websites were largely static brochures consisting of text and simple images. There was very little interaction; users consumed information provided by site owners, but they could not comment, share, or create their own content. It was a digital library in its simplest form.
