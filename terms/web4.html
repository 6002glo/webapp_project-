Web 4.0: The Intelligent Symbiotic Web

Web 4.0 represents the future of our digital lives—an era of "Ultra-Intelligent" connectivity. In this stage, the boundaries between humans and machines begin to dissolve. Through advanced AI, the Internet of Things (IoT), and neural interfaces, the web will become a proactive partner that anticipates user needs before they are even expressed. It is a symbiotic ecosystem where technology is woven seamlessly into the physical world.
