Uniform Resource Locators (URL)

A URL is the precise digital coordinate for a specific resource on the internet. Beyond just being a "web address," a URL specifies the protocol being used (like HTTPS), the domain name, and the specific file path. This structured format ensures that out of the trillions of files online, your browser can pinpoint and retrieve the exact document you are looking for without ambiguity.
