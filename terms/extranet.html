Extranet

An extranet bridges the gap between internal privacy and external collaboration. It is a controlled extension of an intranet that allows specific third parties—such as vendors, partners, or loyal customers—to access a limited portion of a company's network. This allows for secure data exchange and collaborative projects without exposing the company's entire internal infrastructure to the open internet.
