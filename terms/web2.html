Web 2.0: The Participatory Web

Web 2.0 ushered in a revolution of interactivity and social connection. This era turned the web into a "read-write" platform where user-generated content became the focus. The rise of social media, blogs, wikis, and video-sharing sites meant that anyone could be a creator. It transformed the internet from a collection of pages into a vibrant, global community driven by collaboration and real-time data sharing.
